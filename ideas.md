# FESHA Design Brief

## Direction

**The Listening Leaf** — a warm, human editorial-utility direction for a calm first digital step. FESHA should feel like someone sitting beside you and showing one small thing clearly, not like a dashboard or a lesson plan.

## Core principles

- **Voice first:** the microphone is the visual center of gravity and always looks ready to listen.
- **One thing at a time:** every state has one obvious next action, with secondary choices kept quiet.
- **Trust is visible:** Green Shield, last-checked metadata, privacy copy and honest offline labels are part of the main interface.
- **Warm but not childish:** rounded surfaces and illustrated signal shapes create welcome without gamification.
- **Tamil is first-class:** Tamil copy carries the main demo, with short bilingual helper labels only where clarity benefits.

## Color philosophy

- Paper cream `#F7F3EA` keeps the canvas soft and low-glare.
- Deep ink `#20322E` carries text with high contrast.
- Peacock green `#156B57` is the signature trust/action color.
- Leaf mint `#DCEFE6` creates safe surfaces and selected states.
- Marigold `#F3B84B` is reserved for voice energy and first-win moments.
- Coral `#D96F5B` signals a pause or safety stop, never a generic error.
- White `#FFFFFF` is used for cards and clear content grouping.

## Layout paradigm

Use a narrow mobile-first reading column with generous edge padding, one large hero microphone, compact signal chips, and stacked cards. On wider screens, the app becomes a two-column composition: the active conversation and microphone stay on the left while trust, journey and support cards form a quiet utility rail on the right. Keep the bottom navigation thumb-friendly on mobile and move it into a side rail at desktop widths.

## Signature elements

- A leaf-shaped microphone mark, echoing a woman’s voice becoming a new step.
- A warm waveform made of three rounded vertical bars.
- Small green shield badges with a check symbol and “சரிபார்க்கப்பட்டது”.
- Marigold pulse rings around the listening control.
- A simple pointer/halo treatment for guided browser steps.
- Tiny offline and private-session indicators that are always understandable without technical language.

## Interaction and motion

Motion should explain state, not decorate it. The microphone pulse grows only while listening. The waveform moves while understanding. Guidance cards slide in with a short ease-out. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by freezing pulses and using instant state changes. All interactive targets are at least 48px tall and every icon button has visible text or an accessible name.

## Typography system

Use a Tamil-friendly sans-serif stack: `Noto Sans Tamil`, `Nirmala UI`, `Latha`, `Inter`, system sans-serif. Titles are bold, compact and line-height 1.12; body copy is 16–18px with line-height 1.55; labels are 12–13px, never the sole carrier of meaning. Text size controls scale the main content by a small, deliberate range.

## Brand essence and voice

FESHA speaks like a patient older sister: short, clear, respectful, never blaming. It says “பரவாயில்லை. இன்னொரு முறை முயற்சி செய்யலாம்.” instead of “Invalid input.” It celebrates a completed action with “முதல் டிஜிட்டல் படி முடிந்தது!” and “இதை நீங்கள் செய்தீர்கள்.”

## Wordmark and icon

The wordmark is a clean uppercase `FESHA` in deep ink with a small peacock-green leaf mark. The project icon is a full-bleed square peacock-green field with a simple cream hand/leaf microphone silhouette. Avoid gradients, shadows, tiny details, baked-in rounded corners or generated typography inside the icon.

## Signature brand color

Peacock green `#156B57`.
